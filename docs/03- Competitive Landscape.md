03 - Competitive Landscape
Objective

The digital fitness market is highly competitive, with thousands of workout and coaching applications available. However, most existing products fall into one of four categories:

Static workout libraries
AI-generated workout recommendations
Wearable-driven coaching
Human personal coaching

While many products personalize workouts, few combine natural language prompting, continuous adaptation, behavioral coaching, and explainable AI into a single coaching experience.

This analysis identifies where competitors excel and where opportunities remain.
